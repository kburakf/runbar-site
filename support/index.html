Runbar Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests

The fastest way to reach us is to open an issue. Tell us your macOS version, your Runbar version and what you expected to happen.

A service won’t start because its port is in use

Runbar shows which process holds the port, with its PID and folder, and offers to stop it.

My command works in Terminal but not in Runbar

Runbar runs commands with your shell’s environment, so tools like nvm, pyenv and rbenv work. In Settings → Shell environment, check the shell Runbar uses and press Reload after you change your shell’s startup files. Restart running services after a change.

How do I restore Runbar Pro on another Mac?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open Runbar’s Settings and choose Restore Purchase.

Does Runbar collect data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
