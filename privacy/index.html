Runbar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Runbar is made by Baseflow Technology OÜ.

Runbar collects no data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Everything Runbar knows stays on your Mac:

  • The projects you add, their folders and commands are saved in Runbar’s own folder on your Mac.
  • Your commands’ output (logs) is saved there too, and you can delete it at any time.
  • Runbar runs only the commands you added, on your Mac.

Purchases. Runbar Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don’t see your payment details or your Apple ID. Runbar only asks the App Store whether Runbar Pro was bought on this Apple ID.

Contact. Questions about this policy: open an issue.
